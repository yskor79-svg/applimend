UNDERSTAND THE DETAILS.

Understand the repair estimate.

A visit charge and a repair estimate answer different questions. Clarify each with the identified provider before agreeing to the next step.

01

The visit

Ask what the diagnostic charge covers, when it is due and whether any of it is credited if a repair is approved.

02

The proposed repair

Ask for the identified problem, proposed work, parts and labor, and the estimate for the agreed scope.

03

The decision

Understand the estimate, timing, payment and any warranty terms before authorizing work. Clarify how changes to scope will be discussed.

USEFUL QUESTIONS.

What belongs
in the conversation?

These are questions to clarify, not AppliMend rates or service promises.

What is charged for the visit?

Confirm the diagnostic amount, any repair credit and cancellation terms. Ask whether additional appliances are assessed separately.

What does the estimate include?

Clarify the proposed work, parts, labor and any additional charges relevant to the agreed scope.

What if the scope changes?

Agree on how additional findings or work would be explained and approved before proceeding.

What follows the repair?

Confirm the provider’s follow-up contact and any warranty responsibility, duration, coverage and exclusions in writing.
