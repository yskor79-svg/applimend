Common appliance problems

Recognize the symptom and gather the details that help explain it.

REFRIGERATOR

Refrigerator not cooling

Separate a warm refrigerator compartment from a freezer problem. That distinction is more useful than guessing which part has failed.

Refrigerator leaking water

Water inside a compartment and water on the kitchen floor are different symptoms. Note where it first appears.

Read the guide ↗REFRIGERATOR

Ice maker not working

An empty ice bin, small cubes and ice that will not dispense describe different problems.

Washer not draining

Standing water and wet laundry are different observations. Note whether water is actually left in the drum or the load is simply wetter than usual.

Read the guide ↗WASHER

Washer not spinning

Wet laundry may follow a skipped spin, a stopped cycle or a low-spin setting. Describe which you observe.

Dryer not heating

A turning drum does not tell the whole story. Describe whether the dryer produces no noticeable heat or whether drying has simply become slower.

Read the guide ↗DRYER

Dryer takes too long

Compare the usual cycle result with what changed: longer cycles, damp clothes or a cycle that stops early.

Oven not heating

Identify the function that is affected. An oven cavity that will not heat and a single cooktop burner problem should be described separately.

Dishwasher not draining

Describe where the water sits and whether the cycle finished. A photo or a precise description can be more useful than calling every trace of moisture a drainage failure.

Washer leaking water

The location and cycle stage of a leak help explain it. Avoid running another load just to reproduce the problem.

Dryer will not start

A dark display and a display that responds without starting are different symptoms. Include what the controls do.

Read the guide ↗DISHWASHER

Dishwasher not cleaning

Food residue, grit and a cloudy film are different results. Describe what remains after a completed cycle.

Read the guide ↗DISHWASHER

Dishwasher leaking

Water at the door and water beneath the cabinet need different descriptions. Stop using a leaking appliance.

Oven will not turn on

Identify whether the display is dark, the controls are locked or a cooking command receives no response.

Oven temperature not accurate

Slow preheating, uneven baking and a consistent temperature difference are separate observations.

Refrigerator freezing food

About Our Appliance Service

Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you start a request.

Which appliances do you repair?

Refrigerators, freezers and wine coolers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges and cooktops. Microwave requests and household dryer maintenance can also be described; provider suitability must be checked.

Where do you provide appliance service?

Boise, Nampa and Caldwell are the main request cities. Meridian, Eagle, Star, Kuna and Middleton are proposed neighboring areas. City names and ZIPs are not guarantees of technician coverage.

Do I need the model number?

It helps identify the equipment, but you can start with the brand and symptom. Use information you already have; do not move the appliance or remove a panel to find a label.

What should I include about the problem?

Describe what changed, when it began, the affected function and any exact display message. A clear observation is more useful than guessing which part has failed.

Can I describe built-in or stacked equipment?

Yes. Mention the installation so equipment suitability and access can be discussed before a visit.

How can I prepare for an appliance assessment?

Have the model information you already know, a short symptom description and questions about access ready. Do not dismantle the appliance or run extra cycles just to reproduce a fault.

Is the service limited to household appliances?

Yes. Requests are for household kitchen and laundry appliances. Describe the appliance, model and installation so an independent provider can check suitability and availability.
