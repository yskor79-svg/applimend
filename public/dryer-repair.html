Dryer issues we repair

A focused assessment starts with the symptom, the model and the installation.

✓

Will not start

The dryer does not begin a cycle or its controls do not respond.

✓

No heat or damp clothes

The drum turns but the selected heated cycle does not dry normally.

✓

Excessive heat

The appliance or load seems unusually hot. Stop using a dryer with signs of overheating.

✓

Drum does not turn

✓

Noise or vibration

A new squeak, rumble, scraping sound or vibration develops.

✓

Stops during a cycle

The cycle ends early or the dryer switches off unexpectedly.

✓

Codes and flashing lights

A displayed warning or repeated error needs model-specific diagnosis.

✓

Slow or inconsistent starting

The dryer starts intermittently or responds differently from usual.

✓

Clothes emerge unusually wrinkled, damp or unevenly dried.

Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you start a request.

Does a dryer request include vent work?

Not automatically. Appliance diagnosis and any separate vent-related work need to be scoped and confirmed with the provider before a visit. Ask about vent-related work separately.

Is cycle length useful to mention?

Yes. Describe whether the cycle ends at its usual time, runs longer or stops early. Include the selected setting if you know it.

What if I do not know the dryer type?

Leave that detail blank rather than guessing. Include a model number if you already have it and describe the symptoms in plain language.

Which types and brands are included?

We service Electric and gas dryers, Vented and ventless dryers, Front-loading and top-loading units, Stacked laundry combinations, High-efficiency and smart dryers, Household tumble dryers. The brand and exact model help plan the assessment.
