Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you start a request.

Can you tell what’s wrong from the symptoms alone?

Symptoms help describe the request, but they do not establish a diagnosis. The independent provider needs to assess the appliance before proposing a repair.

Should I describe the refrigerator and freezer separately?

Yes. Mention whether one compartment or both seem affected. A separate freezer or another appliance should be identified as a separate item in the description.

What if the problem only happens sometimes?

Describe how often you notice it and what changes when it happens. Intermittent symptoms are still useful information; they do not establish the cause.

Which types and brands are included?

We service French-door refrigerators, Side-by-side refrigerators, Top-freezer refrigerators, Bottom-freezer refrigerators, Built-in refrigerators, Compact and mini refrigerators, Wine coolers. The brand and exact model help plan the assessment.
