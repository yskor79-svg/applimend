Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you start a request.

Which cities are the main request areas?

Boise, Nampa and Caldwell are the primary request cities. Every request still requires provider and appointment confirmation.

Are the neighboring cities confirmed coverage?

No. Meridian, Eagle, Star, Kuna and Middleton are proposed neighboring request areas. Ask for an availability check before relying on a visit.

What if my ZIP is not recognized?

A local ZIP is handled as an availability-check request, never a travel promise. Enter the city as well when discussing the visit; exact coverage is not confirmed by this preview.

Do you cover all of Idaho?

No. The request area is Treasure Valley, centered on Boise, Nampa and Caldwell. Other areas are outside this preview’s regional scope.

Does this form send a request or reserve service?

No. The local demonstration uses sample information and sends nothing. A real request would require a confirmed contact channel and consent before details are shared with an identified provider.
